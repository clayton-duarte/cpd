## 4. Leak and flake risks

1. Every `fx()` test starts a real tmux server through `TestEnv::tmux new-session` (`driver.rs:83`, `driver_reload.rs:74`, `driver_idle.rs:76`, `review_job.rs:75`) and every `plan new` starts one through `cpd`. Each pane runs `sleep 120`/`sleep 600` (`common/mod.rs:75,1092-1102`, `driver.rs:83,287`). Reaping depends on `TestEnv::teardown` (`common/mod.rs:772`, `TEARDOWN_SH` `:116-226`) and on `Drop`; a `SIGKILL` of the test binary leaves the tmux server and its `sleep` (hygiene hole already known: `docs/research/leak-cpdt.md`).
2. Detached drivers: `job dispatch` starts a driver that outlives the call (`driver.rs:195-205`). A test that panics before `wait_end` leaves a driver, a pane and a Worker pi until teardown. Worker shims loop on `until ... attempt show ... running; sleep 0.1` (`driver.rs:175`, `review_job.rs:145`, `driver_idle.rs:147`) ; only `driver.rs:468` (600 counts) and `driver.rs:806` (300 counts) cap the loop, so in the other copies a missing signal spins until teardown (unverified for every copy).
3. Wall-clock and load: `thread::sleep(POLL*N)` absence checks (`driver.rs:730,754`, `driver_reload.rs:304`, `review_job.rs:414`) assert "nothing happened in 100-400 ms". On the Gate at load 40-120 the driver may not have polled at all, so the check passes for the wrong reason (flake the other way: false green). The config checks `waited >= 1000 ms` and `< 2500 ms` (`config.rs:752,767`; `driver.rs:995`) fail at load. `stall_seconds = 2` (`driver.rs:1243,1248`) depends on a 2 s product timer plus `backdate` (`driver.rs:1135`).
4. Timeouts: `WAIT` 30 s and `wait_some` 60 s caps (`common/mod.rs:388`, `driver.rs:216,1205`, `driver_reload.rs:29`) are what the Gate hits at load 120; a hit is a flake, and the pane is still alive then.
5. Ordering: `wait_end` (`driver.rs:225-232`) polls `kill(pid,0)` for driver exit: a pid reused by another process makes it wait to the 30 s cap (unverified).
6. Sockets and dirs: tmux sockets are under the env dir or `/tmp` for a long path (`common/mod.rs:611-616`); `/tmp/tmux-<uid>/cpdt-*` is removed by `teardown` only (`common/mod.rs:225-226`); a crash leaves a socket file in `/tmp`.
7. Shared state: `git_repo` template copy is shared across envs by an atomic `rename` (`common/mod.rs:986-992`): safe, but all repos are copied from one template dir outside the test's temp dir (`git_repo_template`, unverified path).
8. `ask.rs:138,171` spawn `cpd ask` that polls every 500 ms (`core/src/ask/question.rs:53`); `KillOnDrop` reaps it, so no leak, but an answer takes up to 0.5 s of real time (C).
9. Fake `cpd` shims replace the real `cpd` in `PATH` (`plan.rs:44`, `review_close.rs:32`): a shim `exec sleep 120` per pane; none of the shim processes is waited by the test.
10. Not checked: whether `cpd attempt run` in `driver.rs:909-1058` reaches tmux (assumed no, as it refuses before A1); `plan.rs` refusal tests that never start a Lead (assumed from the test names).
