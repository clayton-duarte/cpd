## Method

- **Source.** One read-only `cpd context show --context building-cpd` on 2026-10-20 (745 records, the Manager's hs-d365 to hs-d393 included). None of the 69 older records is superseded yet; hs-d94 (the newer of row hs-d81) already is, by hs-d374.
- **Rule.** A newer record overrules an older one. When the older one still partly holds, the row gives a `restate`: a new decision with what still holds, citing the newer record(s) by id. When it is finished history, the row gives a `retire`: a one-line pointer. Both are written the same way: `cpd context decide --supersedes <older> "<text>"`. A decision may supersede a note (hs-d359 A), so the three note rows (hs-n30, hs-n102, hs-n248) need no special form.
- **Style.** Texts follow the Manager's hs-d365 to hs-d393: a retire starts `Recency (hs-d358, hy-restate 2026-10-20): <older> is out of force`; a restate is titled and says `restated under hs-d358` and what replaced the rest. One line each, no markup.
- **Facts checked.** Where a row turned on a fact (an interim, a shipped PR, a finished batch), I checked it and say what I found in the Action cell. Sources: `git log origin/main` of `~/projects/cpd` (head 1cab019b) and `~/dotfiles`, `gh pr view` / `gh pr list` on clayton-duarte/cpd and clayton-duarte/dotfiles, `cpd plan list`, `cpd plan show --plan cross-repo`, `docs/contract.md` and `docs/commands.md` on this branch, the code they name, and `tmux -L cpd display -p '#{version}'`.
- **Citations.** A text cites records by their ids today. Where it cites a record that another row here restates (for example hs-d305, hs-d42), the id still names the subject; the Manager may swap in the new id when applying.
- **One deviation.** Row hs-d322 / hs-d361 is resolved by restating the **newer** record (hs-d361 carried hs-d266's StatusBar part forward, which the User's later hs-d322 had replaced, and #147 shipped hs-d322). Its command is `--supersedes hs-d361`, not hs-d322. It is marked so in its row.
- **Counts.** 69 rows: 68 in Restate (49 `restate`, 19 `retire`) and 1 in Needs the User. One follow-on retire (hs-d161, the other half of row hs-d143) is given after the table.
