# Protocols

What a Protocol is lives in the **Protocol** entry of [CONTEXT.md](../CONTEXT.md), under `## Checks and gates`. [ADR-0018](adr/0018-protocol-collections.md) records the User's 2026-11-13 clarification and supersedes [ADR-0017](adr/0017-protocols.md); the canonical sentence naming "its protocols" remains in [README.md](../README.md) (air-d85). This registry lists each Protocol, its trigger, application and enforcement requirements. Enforcement descriptions specify intended behavior; this Markdown-only review does not certify implementation coverage. The audit behind the original entries is Plan `protocol-enforce`'s scout artefact (`scout-protocols`, air-n110). The adapter's wire protocol (`PROTOCOL`, commands.md §15) remains a separate sense.

## Guarded rules that are not protocols

The entries above are User-invoked procedures. Rules that are guarded but are not User-invoked procedures — the primary-checkout rule (pi/lib/mainguard.ts), a Worker's refused full test runs (pi/lib/testguard.ts), a Manager's or Lead's refused waits over 30 seconds (pi/lib/noblock.ts), the loop guard (pi/lib/loop-guard.ts), and the `agent_forbidden` and explore-submit refusals (contract §1.2) — are not protocols in this sense. None of them logs a `protocol-breach`; their refusals are `guard` Events (Plan `agent-refusals`).
